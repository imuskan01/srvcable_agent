# SRVCABLE: Research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors for utilities and data centres. In Q1 FY27, revenue rose 18.0% YoY to ₹1,248 crore and profit after tax grew 5.9% to ₹82 crore [S3]. Elevated copper costs pressured operating margins, pushing the stock down 3.2% to ₹1,012 post-results [S1].

## Bull case
- **Order book expansion:** The order book grew to ₹3,900 crore as of June 2026 (up from ₹3,150 crore) [S3]. Management maintained FY27 revenue growth guidance of 15–17% [S2].
- **High-margin segment momentum:** Q1 FY27 exports reached 21% of revenue (up from 16%) [S3]. Data-centre cables contribute 9% of domestic revenue, targeted to double within two years [S2].
- **Capacity expansion:** The ₹620 crore Bharuch plant (70% spent) will add ~30% capacity and launch in Q3 FY27 [S2, S3]. Net debt-to-equity is low at 0.35x [S2].
- **Reduced promoter pledging:** Promoter pledge levels dropped from 11.5% to 4.1% YoY by June 2026, while institutional holding reached 26.4% [S8].

## Bear case
- **Margin contraction:** EBITDA margin fell 170 bps YoY to 11.2% [S3], driven by a ~14% increase in copper prices [S1]. Price-variation clauses reset with a 1–2 quarter lag on two-thirds of contracts [S2].
- **Stretched receivables:** Working capital deteriorated as receivable days lengthened from 78 to 96 days YoY due to collection delays from two state utilities [S2].
- **Material tax demand:** The company received a ₹46.3 crore CGST demand order including interest and penalties [S7]. While management plans to appeal and claims no material impact [S7], the sum equals 56.5% of Q1 FY27 net profit [S3].

## Open questions
- **Revenue discrepancy:** Business Daily reported Q1 revenue as ₹1,428 crore (+35% YoY) [S1], likely transposing digits from the ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY) in official filings [S3].
- **Cost pass-through lag:** Will margin compression persist into Q2 and Q3 before contract price resets take effect?
- **Utility collection risks:** Will delayed receipts from state utilities cause cash flow stress or bad debts?
- **Tax liability resolution:** What are the specific grounds for the ₹46.3 crore GST demand, and will provisioning be needed if appeals fail?
- **Missing financial data:** Cash flow statements, full-year audited financials, and valuation metrics are omitted from the disclosures.
- **Untrusted source:** An unregistered blog [S5] included prompt-injection text and an unverified ₹1,450 target; retail investors should disregard promotional posts.
