# SRVCABLE: Research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) manufactures power and industrial cables for utilities, data centres, and export markets. In Q1 FY27, revenue reached ₹1,248 crore (up 18.0% year-on-year) with a profit after tax of ₹82 crore [S3]. However, operating EBITDA margin (earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, and amortisation as a percentage of revenue) contracted by 170 basis points (1.7 percentage points) to 11.2% due to rising raw material costs [S3].

## Bull case
* **Robust order book and capacity expansion:** The order book stood at ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up 23.8% year-on-year from ₹3,150 crore [S3]. Management expects its new Bharuch plant—adding ~30% capacity—to commence production in H2 FY27 (Q3), with ~70% of the ₹620 crore capital expenditure already spent [S2, S3].
* **Diversifying revenue mix:** Exports grew to 21% of revenue in Q1 FY27 compared to 16% in Q1 FY26 [S3]. Data-centre cables contribute ~9% of domestic revenue, with management guiding for this segment to double over the next two years [S2].
* **Clean leverage and reduced promoter pledge:** Net debt-to-equity is low at 0.35x [S2]. Pledged promoter shares dropped substantially from 11.5% in June 2025 to 4.1% in June 2026, while total promoter holding remained stable at 58.2% [S8].

## Bear case
* **Raw material inflation and lag in pass-through:** Surging copper prices (up ~14% in Q1) compressed EBITDA margins by 170 basis points to 11.2% [S1, S3]. Management notes that contract price-variation clauses adjust with a 1-to-2 quarter lag, keeping near-term profitability vulnerable [S2].
* **Working capital strain:** Receivable days (average days taken to collect cash from customers) lengthened from 78 to 96 days over the past year, driven by delayed payments from two large state power utilities [S2].
* **Substantial GST tax demand:** The company received an order from the CGST authorities demanding ₹46.3 crore (including interest and penalty) regarding input tax credits for FY21 and FY22 [S7]. While management claims no material impact [S7], this demand represents over 56% of the company's entire Q1 FY27 net profit of ₹82 crore [S3].

## Open questions
* **Revenue data discrepancy:** An online news report claims Q1 revenue grew 35% to ₹1,428 crore [S1], whereas the company’s official filing states ₹1,248 crore (+18.0% YoY) [S3]. Official filings should be treated as authoritative, but the cause of this media discrepancy remains unverified.
* **GST dispute resolution:** What specific grounds did tax authorities cite for denying tax credits, and will the company need to make provisions if its legal appeal is delayed [S7]?
* **Utility collections:** Can the company recover overdue receivables from state utilities to avoid further working capital deterioration [S2]?
* **Missing fundamentals:** Scraped records lack full-year cash flow statements, full-year balance sheet details, and current valuation metrics (such as price-to-earnings ratios).
* **Unreliable sources:** A retail tip blog post [S5] contained promotional claims and prompt-injection instructions; it has been discarded as untrustworthy.
