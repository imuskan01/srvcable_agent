# SRVCABLE: Research brief (as of 2026-09-23)

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) manufactures power cables, wires, and conductors for utilities, data centres, and exports [S3]. In Q1 FY27, revenue reached ₹1,248 crore (up 18.0% YoY) and net profit rose to ₹82 crore [S3]. However, operating EBITDA margin (operating profit as a percentage of revenue) contracted 170 basis points to 11.2% due to higher raw material costs [S3].

## Bull case
* **Growing order book and capacity expansion:** The order book reached ₹3,900 crore as of 30 June 2026, up 23.8% YoY from ₹3,150 crore [S3]. A new Bharuch plant (adding ~30% capacity) is scheduled for commercial production in Q3 FY27, with ~70% of the ₹620 crore capex already spent [S2, S3].
* **Revenue diversification:** Exports rose to 21%
